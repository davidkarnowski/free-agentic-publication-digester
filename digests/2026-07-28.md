## Day in Review

The day's Federal Register material was entirely executive and regulatory: 2 presidential documents, 9 final rules, 10 proposed rules, and 65 notices. In the presidential documents, the President approved a proposed agreement for cooperation with Saudi Arabia on peaceful uses of nuclear energy, determining that it will promote common defense and security without unreasonable risk, and authorized the Secretary of State to execute it. The second document directed the Trade Representative to impose tariffs of 10 or 12.5 percent under Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974 on goods from 60 economies, following findings on the enforcement of prohibitions on imports of goods produced with forced labor, with specified product exemptions.

Among the final rules, the Department of Homeland Security amended its regulations to allow USCIS to refer certain affirmative asylum applications to the Executive Office for Immigration Review without an interview; EPA extended certain Toxic Substances Control Act compliance dates for perchloroethylene and carbon tetrachloride and corrected pesticide tolerances for epyrifenacil; NMFS issued Marine Mammal Protection Act regulations for Navy sonar training and testing; and the Department of Veterans Affairs rescinded Veterans Choice Program regulations. The proposed rules included EPA redesignation proposals for three Michigan ozone areas, an OSHA reopening of the benzene record, and comment-period extensions from DOE, CFTC, and NHTSA.

## Terms Used Today

- *engrossed* — the official text of a bill as passed by one chamber
- *enrolled* — the final text of a bill passed by both chambers, sent to the President
- *incorporation by reference* — making an outside document legally part of a rule without reprinting it
- *interim final rule* — a rule that takes effect without waiting for public comment, though comments are still accepted
- *notice of proposed rulemaking* — the formal announcement of a draft regulation
- *proposed rule* — a draft regulation published for public comment before adoption
- *state implementation plan* — a state's federally-approved plan for meeting national air quality standards
