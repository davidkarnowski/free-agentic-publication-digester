The digest carries one presidential document, Proclamation 11054, issued by President Donald J. Trump, designating August 2026 as National Substance Use Primary Prevention Month and calling upon all Americans to unite in the mission of preventing drug use before it begins. Five final rules are also included: the Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau established the Columbia Hills American viticultural area in Washington; the Environmental Protection Agency established a pesticide tolerance exemption for calcium carbonate; the U.S. Department of Agriculture implemented changes for California walnuts, including a schedule for assessment payments and late charges; the Food and Drug Administration reclassified certain in situ hybridization test systems from class III to class II; and the Coast Guard established a temporary safety zone on the Fox River in Green Bay, Wisconsin, for an overwater fireworks display. #### DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

- **Walnuts Grown in California; Changes to Administrative Requirements** (2026-16723; 7 CFR Part 984) — This final rule implements a recommendation from the California Walnut Board (Board) to make changes to the administrative requirements prescribed under the Federal marketing order for walnuts grown in California (Order). This final rule provides a schedule for required handler assessment payments, establishes interest and late payment charges on overdue assessments owed, and modifies the existing reporting requirements for handler acquisitions of walnuts. Action: Final rule. Dates: Effective September 16, 2026.

The safety zone is needed to protect personnel, vessels, and the marine environment from potential hazards associated with an overwater fireworks display. Entry of vessels or persons into this zone is prohibited unless specifically authorized by the Captain of the Port, Sector Lake Michigan, or their designated representative. #### ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY

- **Calcium Carbonate; Exemption From the Requirement of a Pesticide Tolerance** (2026-16720; 40 CFR Part 180) — This regulation establishes an exemption from the requirement of a tolerance for residues of calcium carbonate (CAS # 471-34-1), in or on all food commodities when used in accordance with label directions and good agricultural practices. Columbia River Carbonates, submitted a petition to EPA under the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FFDCA), requesting an exemption from the requirement of a tolerance. This regulation eliminates the need to establish a maximum permissible level for residues of calcium carbonate in or on all food commodities, in accordance with the terms of the exemption. Action: Final rule. Dates: This regulation is effective August 17, 2026. Objections and requests for hearings must be received on or before October 16, 2026, and must be filed in accordance with the instructions provided in 40 CFR part 178 (see also Unit I.C. of this document).
  - *In plain terms:* This rule establishes an exemption from the requirement to set a maximum permissible level for calcium carbonate residues on all food commodities, when used as directed, following a petition from Columbia River Carbonates.
  - Included because: FR-SEL-01 — document type: final rule (all listed)
  - Source: [FR-2026-08-17 / 2026-16720](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/FR-2026-08-17/2026-16720)
