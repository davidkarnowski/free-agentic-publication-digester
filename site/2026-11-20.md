## Day in Review

The digest carries eight recorded roll call votes. In the House, Members voted 401-0 to suspend the rules and pass H.R. 4464, the Preventive Health Savings Act. The Senate rejected H. Con. Res. 93, a War Powers Resolution measure directing removal of U.S. forces from hostilities with Iran, 48-51. It also agreed 48-46 to cloture on Richard Wesley Bennett's nomination to the Southern District of Texas. Separately, the Senate passed H.R. 1520, the Charlotte Woodward Organ Transplant Discrimination Prevention Act, and continued debate on the permitting bill moving as H.R. 2347. The digest also carries two enrolled bills and three public laws bearing earlier approval dates.

On the executive side, the digest carries 20 final rules, four proposed rules, and 87 notices. Executive Order 14438 requires federal contractors to notify the White House of congressional requests about their executive-branch work. A proclamation designates November 2026 as National Adoption Month. The Fish and Wildlife Service proposed listing the Mazama newt as endangered and the White Sands pupfish as threatened. The final rules include several FAA airworthiness directives and DHS implementation of the HAVANA Act.

The digest carries 50 appellate opinions and two national-court decisions. Both national-court decisions are vaccine-program rulings by special masters. In the Fifth Circuit, the court dismissed challenges to EPA's chrysotile asbestos rule for lack of standing. It also vacated a preliminary injunction against Mississippi's Walker Montgomery Protecting Children Online Act. The Federal Circuit affirmed dismissal of Ziklag IP's patent claims against Apple and Amazon Web Services.

## Terms Used Today

- *cloture* — a Senate vote to end debate so a final vote can happen
- *concurrent resolution* — a measure passed by both chambers that does not go to the President and does not have the force of law
- *direct final rule* — a rule that takes effect automatically unless significant objections arrive
- *discharge* — a motion to pull a measure out of committee for floor consideration
- *engrossed* — the official text of a bill as passed by one chamber
- *enrolled* — the final text of a bill passed by both chambers, sent to the President
- *incorporation by reference* — making an outside document legally part of a rule without reprinting it
- *interim final rule* — a rule that takes effect without waiting for public comment, though comments are still accepted
- *motion to proceed* — a Senate vote on whether to start considering a bill
- *notice of proposed rulemaking* — the formal announcement of a draft regulation
- *proposed rule* — a draft regulation published for public comment before adoption
- *safety zone* — a temporary area of water that vessels may not enter without permission
