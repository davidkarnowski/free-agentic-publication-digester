# Free Agentic Publication Digester — Daily Federal Digest

An automated, citation-bound, opinion-agnostic daily digest of official United States government publications — congressional, executive, and judicial — built only from primary sources.

**Today — live** ([today.html](today.html)): watch official publications arrive through the day, newest first (preliminary until the end-of-day digest freezes the record).
