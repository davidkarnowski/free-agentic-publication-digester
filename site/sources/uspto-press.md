The United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO), within the Department of Commerce, examines patent applications and registers trademarks. Its news updates announce intellectual-property policy, operations, and procedural changes.

The United States Patent and Trademark Office is the Commerce Department agency responsible for administering the U.S. patent and trademark systems. USPTO examines patent applications for inventions and grants patents that give inventors exclusive rights; registers trademarks and service marks that identify and protect brand names; maintains searchable databases of patents and trademarks; and enforces the rights associated with patent and trademark ownership. The office serves inventors, businesses, trademark owners, and the public in protecting intellectual property.

The USPTO news-updates channel publishes official announcements about agency policies, operations, and procedural matters. Typical releases address patent examination policy and procedures; patent classification systems and search tools; trademark registration procedures and policy; fee adjustments and payment matters for patent and trademark services; new forms or online systems for patent and trademark applicants; technology and infrastructure updates; and operational notices.

In this digest, you will see USPTO announcements about changes to patent examination guidelines or procedures; updates to trademark application or registration procedures; fee changes for patent and trademark services; new online tools or systems for patent and trademark filing and searching; updates to patent and trademark databases and search capabilities; office technology deployments; and operational notices affecting patent applicants and trademark owners.

USPTO releases are factual statements about intellectual-property administration. The announcements explain how the patent and trademark systems operate and communicate procedural changes to inventors, trademark owners, patent practitioners, and the public. Publications emerge at a rate of a few items per week. The news-updates channel serves as USPTO's primary means of announcing procedural changes and operational updates in patent and trademark administration.
