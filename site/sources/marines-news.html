Model-written orientation

The U.S. Marine Corps publishes news articles and releases about military operations, training exercises, personnel, and organizational matters.

The U.S. Marine Corps is the naval expeditionary service of the Department of Defense and serves as an amphibious and expeditionary warfare force. The Marine Corps is a rapid-response force capable of conducting operations from the sea, maintaining forward-deployed units, and engaging in a range of missions from major combat operations to humanitarian assistance. The Marine Corps operates worldwide and maintains a force structure adapted to expeditionary deployment and amphibious operations.

The Marine Corps' news channel publishes official announcements, press releases, and feature articles covering expeditionary operations, training and exercises, personnel achievements, organizational announcements, equipment and technology initiatives, command changes, humanitarian assistance activities, and strategic priorities. Content includes reports on current and recent operations, notices of military exercises and training activities, announcements of personnel achievements and promotions, organizational changes and unit activations, equipment modernization or weapons system development, international cooperation initiatives, and feature stories about Marines and units. The publication frequency is high, with multiple items published daily.

Items appearing in this digest from the Marine Corps will reflect topics the service has selected for public communication. These may include expeditionary operations and deployments, training and readiness exercises, personnel and command announcements, equipment acquisitions or modernization programs, organizational changes, international partnerships and exercises, humanitarian assistance activities, technology development, strategic doctrine and policy statements, or unit and individual achievements. The Marine Corps' news channel provides official Marine Corps perspectives on its operations and priorities.

The Marine Corps' communications reflect the service's role as an expeditionary and amphibious force. The news channel serves as a formal channel for the Marine Corps to communicate with Congress, media, the public, allies, and stakeholders about its operations, capabilities, and organizational matters.
