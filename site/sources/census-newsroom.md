The U.S. Census Bureau is a federal statistical agency within the Department of Commerce responsible for collecting, analyzing, and disseminating data on the nation's population, economy, housing, and businesses. The Bureau conducts a wide range of surveys and administrative data collection activities, from the decennial Census of Population and Housing to ongoing monthly surveys of employment, retail sales, manufacturing output, international trade, and many other economic indicators. Census data forms the statistical foundation for congressional redistricting, federal funding formulas, business planning, academic research, and public understanding of national demographic and economic conditions.

The Census Bureau's press-release index announces the timing and availability of major data releases produced by its programs. The index typically contains one or more announcements per business day, as the Bureau releases different datasets on a published schedule. Releases provide advance notice of when data will be publicly available, context about what the data measures, and information about how to access it. Announcements cover releases from economic surveys (employment data, retail sales, manufacturing), demographic programs (population estimates, housing statistics), business data (international trade, foreign investment), and specialized surveys. The Bureau uses its newsroom to coordinate the timing and public availability of its statistical products and to provide guidance on interpreting statistical releases.

In the digest, Census Bureau announcements represent the official schedule and availability of key national statistics. Readers encounter notice of when new economic and demographic data become public, what topics the data covers, and where to find it. These releases serve as official documentation of when statistical information is becoming available to the public, researchers, businesses, and policymakers. The announcements provide access to some of the most widely cited federal statistics on the nation's economy, population, and demographic characteristics.
