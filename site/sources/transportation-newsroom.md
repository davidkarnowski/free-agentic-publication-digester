**Model-written orientation**

The Department of Transportation publishes press releases on national transportation policy, infrastructure funding, and safety regulations across all transportation modes.

The Department of Transportation (DOT) is the federal executive agency responsible for developing and implementing national transportation policy. It oversees highway transportation through the Federal Highway Administration, aviation through the Federal Aviation Administration, railroads through the Federal Railroad Administration, maritime transportation through the Maritime Administration, and pipeline safety through the Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration. DOT also houses the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), which sets vehicle safety standards. Together, these administrations regulate transportation infrastructure, set safety standards, distribute federal funding for transportation projects, and enforce transportation law across the United States.

Through its departmental newsroom, DOT publishes official press releases and announcements on national transportation policies, safety regulations, infrastructure funding, and major departmental initiatives. Readers will find press releases covering topics such as grant announcements for transportation infrastructure projects, final safety regulations affecting vehicles or transportation infrastructure, enforcement actions against transportation operators or violations of transportation law, policy changes affecting transportation modes or industries, transportation infrastructure assessments, leadership statements, and major departmental initiatives.

DOT releases typically include details about funding amounts, affected jurisdictions, implementation timelines, regulatory requirements, and statements from departmental officials. The releases serve as official notification to state and local transportation authorities, the transportation industry, workers in transportation sectors, and the traveling public of federal transportation policy decisions. Individual modal administrations within DOT maintain separate communication channels and publish their own technical and operational announcements, but the departmental newsroom publishes releases addressing policies affecting the transportation system broadly.
