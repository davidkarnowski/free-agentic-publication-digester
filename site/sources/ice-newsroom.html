Model-written orientation

U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), a DHS agency, publishes official news releases about interior enforcement operations and investigations.

U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) is a component of the Department of Homeland Security responsible for enforcing federal immigration and customs laws in the interior of the United States. Established in 2003, ICE operates two principal divisions: Enforcement and Removal Operations, which focuses on identifying individuals subject to immigration enforcement, and Homeland Security Investigations, which investigates financial crimes, human trafficking, smuggling, intellectual property theft, and other violations related to national security and public safety.

ICE's newsroom publishes official announcements about the agency's operations, enforcement activities, and investigative work. Content includes press releases describing enforcement operations, details of significant investigations and prosecutions, updates on policy matters affecting the agency's operations, and notices about ICE's work. The newsroom is updated several times per week with new releases.

Items appearing in this digest from ICE's newsroom will reflect topics the agency chooses to communicate publicly. These may include specific enforcement operations, arrests and prosecutions, policy announcements, organizational updates, partnerships with other agencies or international partners, technology deployments, or personnel changes. Readers should understand that ICE's newsroom represents the agency's own official communications and is not an independent audit of its activities.

The newsroom index serves as the primary public-facing publication channel for ICE's official statements, making it a direct source for understanding what the agency communicates publicly about its operations and work.
