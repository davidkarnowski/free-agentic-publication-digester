Model-written orientation

Oversight.gov operates as a central aggregator of audit, inspection, and investigative reports from the federal Inspectors General offices. It republishes reports from roughly 70 Inspector General organizations across the federal government.

Oversight.gov is operated by the Council of the Inspectors General on Integrity and Efficiency (CIGIE) as a centralized repository and aggregation point for federal oversight and audit activities. CIGIE is a council composed of Inspectors General from across the federal government. Oversight.gov serves as a unified portal for accessing audit, inspection, and investigative reports produced by the federal Inspector General community.

The site aggregates and republishes audit, inspection, and investigative reports from approximately 70 federal Offices of Inspector General distributed across executive-branch agencies, legislative-branch agencies, and the federal judiciary. Each Inspector General office operates independently within its respective organization and conducts audits, inspections, and investigations specific to that agency's operations, programs, and finances. Oversight.gov brings these independent reports together into a single searchable repository.

Reports appearing on oversight.gov cover federal program audits assessing efficiency and effectiveness of government operations and federal spending; inspections and reviews of federal agencies, programs, and facilities; investigative reports on fraud, misconduct, mismanagement, and waste within the federal government; and evaluations of specific federal initiatives, policies, and programs. Reports typically include findings, analyses of issues identified, and recommendations for corrective action or improvement.

While these reports are aggregated and republished on oversight.gov for centralized access, each report originates from an individual Inspector General office, and the originating Inspector General serves as the authoritative source and owner of the report. The aggregation reflects the oversight.gov mission of making federal oversight activities transparent and accessible. New reports are posted to oversight.gov most business days as Inspector General offices complete and release their work. Readers will encounter independent federal accountability and oversight documentation reflecting the collective work of the Inspector General community across all branches of the federal government.
