Model-written orientation

The Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service publishes stakeholder bulletins on regulatory actions, agricultural pest and disease emergencies, and program announcements.

The Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service (APHIS), a division of the Department of Agriculture, is responsible for protecting U.S. agriculture from the introduction and spread of animal and plant pests and diseases. The agency administers regulations and programs governing animal imports, international trade in animals and plants, domestic animal and plant health safeguards, and the control of invasive species.

APHIS email bulletins carry regulatory announcements, emergency notices, and program updates for farmers, agricultural businesses, veterinarians, and other stakeholders. Readers of the digest will see announcements of new or modified regulations affecting agricultural practices, permitting requirements, or trade; notices of disease or pest detections or outbreaks requiring managed response; emergency orders or quarantines imposed to contain or prevent spread of agricultural threats; and announcements of programs such as grant funding, training, or technical assistance related to animal and plant health.

The agency operates in response to disease and pest events, so some announcements address time-sensitive situations—an emerging pest in a particular region, a disease case confirmation requiring notification to affected communities or industries, or a change in import or movement restrictions. Other announcements reflect ongoing program management or regulatory changes.

APHIS works across the nation's agricultural regions and also manages border and import inspections to prevent the entry of foreign pests and diseases. The agency coordinates with state agriculture departments, industry groups, and international partners. Readers of this digest will see the breadth of the agency's regulatory and emergency-response work affecting agriculture.

Items appear in the digest as APHIS publishes them through the email channel and do not backfill earlier announcements or publications not distributed through the subscription.
