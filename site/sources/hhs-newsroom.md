The Department of Health and Human Services publishes departmental press releases and announcements on federal health programs and public health policy.

The Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) is the federal executive agency responsible for administering health and human services law and programs. It is the largest federal health agency and houses multiple divisions including the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), the Food and Drug Administration (FDA), the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS), the National Institutes of Health (NIH), the Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA), and others. Together, these agencies oversee a vast portfolio of federal activities including disease surveillance and response, food and drug safety, Medicare and Medicaid programs, medical research, and mental health and substance abuse treatment programs.

Through its departmental newsroom, HHS publishes official press releases and announcements on policies, initiatives, and major actions affecting federal health programs and public health. Readers would encounter press releases covering topics such as new health regulations or policy guidance, responses to public health matters, program funding announcements, Medicare and Medicaid policy changes, enforcement actions related to health care fraud or regulation, leadership announcements, and major departmental initiatives addressing health care access, costs, or outcomes.

The departmental newsroom serves as the official channel for HHS-level policy announcements and leadership statements. Individual agencies within HHS—including the CDC, FDA, and others—maintain separate communication channels for their specialized work and more frequent updates within their specific areas. The departmental releases reflect decisions at the secretary level and policies affecting multiple HHS components or the health care system broadly.
