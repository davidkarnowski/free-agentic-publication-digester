## Day in Review

The digest includes two presidential proclamations, designating October 2026 as National Energy Dominance Month, which describes various energy policy actions, and October 12, 2026, as Columbus Day. Eighteen final rules were also published. Among these, the FAA adopted airworthiness directives for certain aircraft engines, and the FDA issued a rule to permit salt substitutes in standardized foods to reduce sodium content. The EPA approved Colorado's application for primary enforcement responsibility for Class VI injection wells and extended a compliance date for Clean Water Act Hazardous Substance Facility Response Plans. Fisheries rules involved Pacific cod reallocations off Alaska and an announcement regarding Maine's 2026 summer flounder commercial quota. Multiple Coast Guard safety zones were established on the Chicago River for bridge repair. Additionally, the digest carries six proposed rules, including NMFS fishery specifications and CBP advance electronic traveler information requirements for rail carriers.

Four appellate court opinions from the Fifth Circuit are also featured. The court dismissed appeals in *USA v. Malik* and *USA v. Peacock* as frivolous, remanding the latter to correct a clerical error. It affirmed the conviction of a chiropractor in *USA v. Tekippe* for health care and wire fraud, and affirmed the dismissal of a federal lawsuit in *Ratcliff v. Old American* due to non-compliance with sanctions and lack of jurisdiction.

## Methodology

Selection rules, summarization prompts, and thresholds are versioned in this
repository and identified by the pipeline version in the header (54604c76).
Editorial principles — primary sources only, opinion-agnostic prose, mechanical
party-blind selection, full coverage accounting — are defined in
[GUIDE.md](../GUIDE.md) §2. Ruleset in effect: prompt version 2; plain-language version 2. To reproduce this digest: re-run the
report stage against the extracted records for 2026-10-13; no upstream re-fetch
is required (GUIDE.md §5).

*Inference (GUIDE §6 r15, standing): The pipeline finalizes every
publication day with or without an inference provider. Model layers
are additive. When no inference was available for a day, the digest
states that fact in its own prose and nothing more; the cause is
operational detail recorded in the day's provenance and operations
report, not in the published digest. The Coverage Statement's
arithmetic reconciles regardless. Items are listed with their
citations whether or not they were summarized. A day finalized
without model layers is frozen like any other day; prose is not
backfilled into a frozen digest.*

*Filing note (2026-08-06, standing): digests from 2026-08-06 file
govinfo packages under their day of first observation — FAPD's three
clocks are explained in the [FAQ](faq.html#fapds-three-clocks). The
Federal Register files under its cover date, on which it is legally
published. Digests before 2026-08-06 filed by each document's own
date; the two Congressional Record issues observed 2026-08-04/05
(proceedings of 08-03/08-04) fell between the freeze and this change
and appear in no digest — disclosed here, not backfilled.*

*"In plain terms" lines are model-generated restatements of the stored
