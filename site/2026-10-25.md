## Day in Review

The digest carries 28 appellate court opinions, 61 district court opinions, and one bankruptcy court opinion. On October 23, 2026, the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit, sitting en banc in an opinion by Judge Wilson, reversed district court decisions and vacated habeas grants to three noncitizens detained under 8 U.S.C. § 1225(b)(2)(A) pending removal proceedings. It held that the petitioners were not entitled to individual hearings on dangerousness or flight risk before detention. The digest also carries the July 2, 2026 panel opinion by Judge Southwick in the same consolidated appeals, which had affirmed the grants on due process grounds.

It denied an immigration petition for review in Iraheta v. Blanche. Its other opinions include criminal affirmances and several dismissals under Anders v. California.

The First Circuit affirmed RICO conspiracy and drug conspiracy convictions of Luis H. Quiñones-Santiago, Eduardo Rosario-Orangel, and Avelino Millán-Machuca. That opinion appears under multiple captions and dates. In United States v. Barros Brandao, the First Circuit affirmed a conviction for false statements on a passport application. backfilled into a frozen digest.*
